Coin Dash

A simple collection game concept: reach a coin in ten seconds, reset the timer on collection, and face fewer, farther coins as the score rises.

The decision: guarantee at least one reachable coin after each reset while letting the player choose which to chase.

My role: chose the game and clarified the fairness rule. AI assisted the research page and Python placement probe.

ObservedIn the recorded 5,000-layout model, guarded placement always left a reachable coin; random placement failed that condition 162 times.

Contribution, learning & limits

The probe checks a flat arena, constant speed, a ten-second timer, and an assumed two-second reaction reserve. Its assertions cover coin counts, positions, fallback cases, and distance boundaries.

Design takeaway: write a fairness condition that can be checked before choosing the final tuning.

Current limit: a mathematical sample is not a human playtest. The Godot game, Blender assets, accounts, and saved scores are not implemented. Gameplay work is paused until I return to the Windows setup.

Next: build a primitive arena, then measure whether a player can judge and reach one of the last two coins.

An animated-series museum

An AI-assisted IS117 digital exhibit that organizes material about Batman: The Animated Series into a themed, browsable website.

The decision: use sections and anchor navigation to guide a visitor through the exhibit’s topics.

My role: the project README describes my direction of the design and revisions, with ChatGPT and AI image-generation assistance.

ObservedThe published HTML matches the repository source, and its internal navigation targets exist.

Contribution, learning & limits

The current source uses HTML, embedded CSS, and JavaScript for themed sections, responsive rules, and scrolling effects. This case study points to inspectable code rather than attributing a backend to it.

Learning focus: connect a visual theme to a readable content hierarchy while documenting AI assistance.

Current limit: source/link checks do not establish browser accessibility or verify every historical claim and image’s provenance. A keyboard and reduced-motion review remains to do.

Next: try the full exhibit on a phone and keyboard, then document an observed issue and repair.
